# Pre-confirmation claim ledger

This ledger is frozen before external-transfer or official-validation metrics
are read. It records only the completed clean/dev evidence from R0, R1.5,
R2-Core, R2-D, and R3-P. External confirmation may narrow scope but may not
create a new method claim or reopen model development.

## Supported

- Direct 64-sample M2 stochastic pushforward improves downstream trajectory
  energy relative to deterministic and M1 Gaussian propagation on the frozen
  clean and lowlight/dropout/beam dev authorities under both CV and MLP hosts.
- The accepted stochastic pushforward improves future-error identification
  without obtaining its energy gain by enlarging equal-coverage regions.

## Bounded or local positive

- R1.5 found a DSE-over-concat/MC quality--efficiency advantage on its frozen
  seeds, but R2-Core did not reproduce a stable capacity-independent or
  distribution-family mechanism. The R1.5 result is therefore local evidence,
  not the paper's method claim.
- R2-D compressed the MC future distribution, risk ordering, and sharpness
  into one forward pass with a 38.55x measured speedup, but the identical
  GT-only student was better on trajectory energy and diagnostic mixture NLL.
- R3-P found a narrow clean risk--coverage gain for the M2 token over mean and
  confidence controls, without a primary trajectory-energy gain.

## Not supported

- A stable heavy-tail or circular-yaw causal mechanism for learned DSE gains.
- A stable capacity-independent necessity of DSE.
- Distribution-preserving distillation as a necessary forecasting objective.
- Explicit M2 probability-token necessity under ordinary GT supervision on
  the primary trajectory energy score.
- Strong-host escalation, a new propagation theory, or further RiskProp3D
  rescue-model development.

## Frozen central boundary

Perception distributions have downstream value under explicit stochastic
pushforward, but that value does not automatically make explicit probability
representations necessary for ordinary GT-supervised forecasting. Any residual
learned value is currently narrower and more evident in risk ranking than in
primary trajectory-distribution quality.

Official validation and internal test have not been read for this study at
this freeze point.
